Privacy & Your Data

Simple, transparent rules about what we store and what we never ask for.

What we store

  • Your sign-in email or phone number and an Argon2-hashed password.
  • Your household setup, income schedule, recurring bills, savings goals, daily spending entries, plans, and weekly/monthly reviews.
  • Temporary demo accounts created via "Try the demo" expire automatically after 24 hours.

What we never collect or log

  • Never bank account numbers, debit or credit card numbers, BVN, or NIN.
  • Never third-party tracking ads or behavioural profiles.
  • Application logs never record your name, email, phone number, or Naira amounts.

How to export or delete everything

When signed in, open Settings to download all your data in CSV or JSON format at any time, or permanently delete your account and every associated record.
